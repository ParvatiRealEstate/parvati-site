Where our clients live

Financial District

Quiet in the evenings and at weekends, with converted office buildings, river views and nearly every subway line close by.

Tribeca

Cobblestone streets and loft conversions in old warehouses, with the Hudson River Park a short walk west.

Upper West Side

Pre-war co-ops and brownstone blocks between two parks, Central Park and Riverside Park.

Upper East Side

Tree-lined side streets, townhouses and doorman buildings, a few blocks from Central Park and the museums on Fifth Avenue.
